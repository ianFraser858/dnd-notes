## Session Overview
The campfire conversation with **Tupi Lulu** wrapped up. He told the party what he knows about the memory demon: it lairs underground nearby, is most active at night, and is at roughly 60% strength. He offered a deal: help him kill it, and he'll help with one of their problems — the coming demons or the mind flayers. He also made clear **he will not reset Keepsake again**. ## Session Summary

### Part 1: Housekeeping — the Halloween One-Shot
Joey announced the theme for this year's Halloween game: **a zombie and its ghost come from the same body, and hilarity ensues.** Light-hearted, mostly fighting zombies, with **3rd-level characters** (zombies aren't threatening much past level 3). It's planned for **Saturday, October 31**. Halloween falling on a Saturday matches the group's first Halloween one-shot in 2020.

One player asked to play a ghost whose own zombie body the party is fighting ("some goddamn necromancer raised my body without my permission"). Joey said that fits the plot and to send him an ancestry; an archetype or a summoner build with the body as the summon were floated. Joey is working Halloween (a theater job) but expects to start around the usual time.

### Part 2: Out-of-Character Q&A
Ian had questions about last session's info dump, most of which had gone to Ardryl. Joey's answers:

- **Hellmouths:** Tupi Lulu travels between worlds through them. The **memory demon works for the demons** behind the hellmouths. It is *sent* for one of three reasons (vanguard, reckoning, message), rather than taking three forms.
- **Is there a hellmouth on this planet?** Not known. The table assumes there must be one somewhere, since the demon got here.
- **The Ring City is unrelated to the hellmouth.** The mind flayers were already here when "the French dude" (**Ludo La France**, per Emily — Joey said the pronunciation was slightly off) arrived. He came from a world that had been overrun by a full demonic invasion, well past the memory-demon stage. On arrival he saw the mind flayers, decided they were cool, and became one. Why exactly is in another of Joey's four notebooks.
- **The resets:** resetting a small area is Tupi Lulu controlling his curse. The curse itself would be reliving the same day forever. For **hundreds of years** the curse had him **eaten by the demon every day** before he got even a little control, and several hundred more to get what control he has now. He should have broken long ago; that he didn't comes down to willpower, anger, or hate. He is "not a hundred percent all there." He doesn't know what kind of demon cursed him.
- **Why limit resets:** resetting the same small area over and over has **very bad consequences for the people in it**. (Ominous thunder.)

Emily added from her notes: Ardryl is the de facto leader in Tupi Lulu's eyes; the party is the only difference from his last run; the town dies in 36 hours; and the demon's victims are simply forgotten. She also worked out the horror of a "message" city: everyone would remember the city, but not the people in it — a great empty city, and the dawning terror of not knowing whether you knew anyone there. Joey added that Tupi Lulu himself only remembers being **a hunter for his village**. He remembers his own role but nothing of the people.

### Part 3: Questions for Tupi Lulu
Back at the fire, in character:

- **The goal:** kill it. He has wounded it a few times. *"I've seen it bleed, which means I can kill it."* Until now it has been one person against all that power.
- **Why not get help?** It starts in small villages where people can barely fire a crossbow, and they just die. *"Most planets aren't blessed with people of your caliber"* — and after a few days nobody remembers anyone was taken.
- **What it looks like:** Joey's stand-in for now is a **Khezu** from Monster Hunter. Ugly, but it has the best theme song in the series.
- **Who has seen it:** generally only the people it eats. *"Some people"* — Joey looked straight at Daniel — *"get lucky and are strong enough to at least fight it off for a little bit."* **Ardryl has seen the demon.** That's what happened behind the door the night he lost half his HP and his memory of it.
- **Where it lairs:** somewhere **underground, nearby**. It is most active **at night** because it isn't strong yet. Finding the lair is the job for the next 24 hours. The party jumped on **the mines**: in a mining town they haven't met a single miner. Either the miners were the first ones eaten or the demon is down there.
- **How it hunts:** sneaky rather than head-on.
- **Weaknesses, resistances, immunities:** none he knows of. Joey refused the resistances question at first, then explained that Tupi Lulu can't test everything.
- **Strength:** hellmouth travel weakens it, which is why it starts with small villages before cities. Keepsake is its third village. It's at about **60% strength** now, and finishing Keepsake would bring it to about **75%**.

### Part 4: The Deal
Asked about his plans afterward: he'll **find the demon that cursed him and kill it**. The memory demon is that demon's lieutenant.

The party asked for help with their own demons — the flying city overhead. Tupi Lulu asked if they were sure the actual demons about to invade weren't the bigger deal. The party suggested getting the two to fight each other. His offer:

> *"If you help me kill this fucker, I'll help you with one of your problems."*

That means either the invading demons or the flying city. On learning the flying city is **illithids**: *"Oh, that's a rough one."* The party summed up their position as an unstoppable force and an immovable object.

### Part 5: No More Resets
The party asked whether he could, if it came to it, reset to a point after they arrived. ***"I'm not resetting."*** He has used his resets on Keepsake.

Joey spelled out the stakes out of character:
- The 36 hours is the window to **save Keepsake and its people**.
- If the party fails here, there is **one more village** before the demon reaches a big city like **Hammerhead**. Once it reaches Hammerhead, it's over.

### Part 6: The Night
The conversation had taken about an hour, leaving roughly **35 hours**. The party decided to get a proper 8-hour rest. Tupi Lulu advised sleeping in the forest, not the village: *"Unless you want to wake up in the belly of a memory demon, I would suggest sleeping out here."* Questions about the mines would have to wait for morning — the village was asleep.

**Healing:** Treat Wounds brought **Ardryl back to full**, and **Moz** (who was 12 down) back to full with a 13. Moz has a +0 Con modifier.

The party cooked some of the dozen rabbits; Tupi Lulu isn't offended — he looks like a rabbit, but isn't one. Sierra asked about tactics for checking the mines for enemies; no plan yet. Sam also asked whether anyone had the pyramid rifles. **All the guns were handed over to Warp** back at base.

Tupi Lulu offered to take first watch. On the night's Perception checks, **Moz (40) and Jack** each heard **faint screams from the direction of the village** during their watches. Even a ten-minute dead sprint wouldn't have made a difference; Moz let it go. He tried to run through everyone they'd met in town to see if he'd forgotten anyone. Joey: *"I'm not gonna tell you."*

### Part 7: Morning
A gray dawn with fog in the air and mist on the leaves after a heavy night. Moz had last watch. Tupi Lulu skinned and cooked rabbit with berries for breakfast — excellent, not gamey; three thousand years alone teaches you to cook.

He stayed at the forest's edge rather than going into town: a six-foot-tall, six-armed rabbit-like creature with spiraling arms draws attention, and he isn't used to people anymore. The party can signal him when needed.

### Part 8: The Smiths
Jack led the party to the town's two smithies, which sit across the street from each other.

**The armorsmith, Francis** (an elf; "Francis" is a common name he goes by, not his real one):
- A few of the mines produce metal, but **most are gem mines** — one metal mine, the rest gems. Gems sell to big-city clerics for rituals.
- He doesn't do much business with the miners and doesn't source metal locally.
- To buy gems, see **Saris at the general store**.
- No runesmith in town — nobody local was ever taught. With a pattern from the general store he could rough something out, but someone would still have to imbue it. Maybe Mayor Kato knows how (heard as "Maricado").
- Pointed them to the sister shop across the street.

**The weaponsmith, Misty** (a dwarf woman):
- They used to get ore locally, but **metal ore has dried up**. The mines mostly produce **gemstones** now, which go to bigger cities like **Hammerhead** in exchange for metal ingots.
- One mine still brings up a little metal, mostly **copper** — poor for weapons. She'd love some mithral.
- She made the **mayor's ceremonial sword**, gem-encrusted. It was last used for the ribbon-cutting when **Saris renovated the general store** a few years ago.
- Occasional special orders from rich people in Hammerhead who want gems "authentic to the village they were dug up in."
- **Ruby runs the bar by herself** and has for a few years. It's a big place for one person, but Ruby says she can handle it.

### Part 9: Emily Notices
Comparing what the smiths said with her notes, Emily realized the town no longer matched them. Joey played it straight: *"Everything's fine. Everything's fine."* Asked about Greg with two G's: *"There is no one named Greg with two G's in my notes. I don't know who you're talking about."* According to the town, there is no Tim, the general store is Saris's, and the armory is Francis's.

### Part 10: Deputy Mayor Mark
At the mayor's office (the biggest building in town), the receptionist — a Greg — said **Mayor Kato is busy**: there's a **shortage of crop workers**, and he's meeting with the head farmers on rotation. The party could see the deputy mayor instead.

Behind the desk sat a **goblin** with a plaque reading **Deputy Mayor Mark** — the goblin who was talking with Kato at the bar on the first night.
- First remark: the party's armor is "a little bit too good for around here."
- **Most of the miners are currently working the crop problem.** The village has had **about forty people leave recently — 20% of the population**. Nobody told him where they went, "better opportunity," presumably. He's only deputy mayor; he steps in if something happens to Kato.
- The party may poke around the mines. They're structurally stable; don't knock out support beams, and put back any pickaxe or mine cart you move. Mostly gems.

Out of character, Emily said Jack only *knows* something is wrong because they were told so — Mark's "people moved away" sounds harmless on its face. But Tupi Lulu said people are being eaten, and the party heard screams last night. **Nobody in town has mentioned screams**, though the party hasn't asked.

### Part 11: The Mine Entrance
The party signaled Tupi Lulu, who met them at the **first mine entrance**. Outside it:
- **Well-cared-for tools** — pickaxes, shovels, a couple of drills
- **Explosives**, properly stored
- A large **canary enclosure**, with the birds still being looked after

It's a properly run mine. Joey settled the number of mine tunnels at **three** (he'd said four, Emily remembered two). The session ended at the entrance.

About forty villagers (20%) count as having "moved away," and a crop-labor shortage has pulled the miners into the fields. In town memory Saris has always owned the general store and Ruby has always run the bar alone.
- **The characters don't remember either.** The missing are gone from the party's memories in-game too. Only the players (and Emily's notes) know.
- **Ardryl fought off the memory demon** the night he lost half his HP. Survivors who see it are rare.
- **Lair: underground, nearby; active at night.** The mines are the prime suspect: a mining town with no miners around, metal ore that "dried up," and a mayor who already planned to use the mines as a shelter.
- **Keepsake's mines are mostly gem mines** (one produces copper). The gems go to big-city clerics and to Hammerhead in trade for ingots.
- **Demon strength: ~60% now, ~75% after Keepsake.** It's weakest early, after hellmouth travel.
- **No more resets.** Tupi Lulu is out of resets for Keepsake. Fail here and there is one more village before Hammerhead, after which it's too late.
- **Tupi Lulu's past:** he spent hundreds of years being eaten by the demon daily under the curse before gaining control. He remembers only that he was his village's hunter; the people are gone from his memory.
- **The Ring City and the hellmouths are separate threats.** The mind flayers were here before Ludo La France arrived from a demon-overrun world.
- **The memory demon is a lieutenant** of the fiend that cursed Tupi Lulu.
- **No runesmithing in Keepsake.**
