## Session Summary

### Part 1: Combat at the Forge
Combat picked up from last session's cliffhanger. The dog-turned-giant-robot took a defensive stance with its glaive across the middle of the room. Horbach hung back near the forge portal.

**Round 1:**

**Round 2:**
- **Robot:** Walked in and swung the massive glaive at Zarius. **Crit — 60 slashing** → **55 after Sierra's DR 5.** Zarius bloodied badly.

Old Horbach: *"wide, serene, god-like entity."* This Horbach: **"sniveling coward, kind of just doing whatever he was told."** The perceptions do not line up.

## Lore Discoveries

- **Horbach is under mind-flayer control, not a willing collaborator.** Billy Butler confirmed the pattern: mind-flayer control has to be re-upped every few days; keep him separated for a few days or kill his controller and it breaks. Ardryl's own observation matched — the Horbach at the forge was "sniveling and doing whatever he was told," nothing like the "wide, serene, god-like entity" they met at the Throne of the Mountain King. **Last session's assumption was wrong.** He was worth rescuing all along.

- **The mind-flayer construct line uses Greek-letter naming.** The robot the party fought is designated **Omicron Beta** — built on the Omicron chassis (the "big bulky one," which Tau previously told them about). Schematics show planned iterations: reduce size, increase firepower → **Omicron Gamma**. Other classes named include **Kappa** and **Gamma** ("the wild weezy one — we gotta be careful around that"). This is a whole product line.

- **Omicron Beta is a fusion design.** Party inferred it may combine Tau's chassis (dog form) with Omicron's transforming/glaive capability. Design goal: unparalleled stealth + terrifying combat prowess. Stealth unverified — party only saw it for ~30 seconds before Horbach and it disappeared.

- **The Governor's Manor has a hidden escape tunnel.** Master bedroom → servants' walkways → out. Confirms Ivan's intel (2026-03-10) that an escape route exists. This is how Little got away.

- **Governor Little's treaty of fealty is a real, physical document.** The party recovered only page one, but it exists in written form and can presumably be used to publicly expose Little.

- **A legal path to a citizens militia exists in Silverleaf.** Zarius found a valid legal precedent. Political tool — hasn't been used yet.

- **The teleporter room in the pyramid links to the Governor's Manor.** The "GOVERNOR"-labeled pad the party spotted last week presumably links there; the previously-inactive third pad also deposited them there this week. There's a real teleporter web between key mind-flayer nodes.

- **The forge had a hype-metal music system.** Not campaign-critical but confirmed. Horbach was forging to a soundtrack.

- **The White Rabbit is a leading indicator.** Every city where the White Rabbit has been sighted has subsequently fallen to the mind flayers. Number of sighting cities is growing. Party does not yet know what the White Rabbit *is* — only the correlation.
