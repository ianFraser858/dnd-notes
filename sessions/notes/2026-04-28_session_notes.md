# Pathfinder Session Notes - April 28, 2026

## Session Overview
The party, now joined by the Nephilim Ursa, buffed up and launched a second assault on the two gargoyle guardians protecting the blue pyramid's entrance. A critical mid-combat discovery changed the fight entirely: the gargoyles can revive each other, meaning both must be downed in the same round or the fight never ends. After multiple grueling rounds, Moz's fireball caught both gargoyles below 10 HP simultaneously, clearing the way into the pyramid.

## Session Summary

### Part 1: Pre-Combat Setup
The party arrived back at the pyramid entrance. The DM re-established the scene: a wide staircase leading up to the blue pyramid, flanked by two gargoyle statues - the only known entrance. Ursa stood ready with his massive glaive.

Before engaging, the party buffed up just outside the gargoyles' aggro range. A "haste circle" was arranged: Zarius cast haste on Moz, Moz cast haste on Ardryl - giving both casters an extra action for the fight. Moz also cast a rank 4 spell granting greater invisibility (and some DR) for the combat ahead. Ardryl designated the blue gargoyle as hunted prey.

Ursa summarized his combat approach when asked: he gestured to his enormous glaive. When asked about ranged options, he gestured to the glaive again.

### Part 2: Round 1 - Opening Salvo
The gargoyles animated and hopped down the steps the moment the party stepped forward. Sierra went first (initiative 40 - rolled a 21 on the die, stunned the table), but her commander abilities required reactions no one had yet, so she passed.

Ardryl opened with the hunted shot combination (one hit, 16 damage to blue), then an assisting shot to give the next attacker a +1 circumstance bonus to hit blue. He added warden's boon to bring down the penalty on Ursa's secondary attacks.

Ursa charged blue with his whirling glaive, scoring a crit (roughly 48 damage) and sweeping an area of fire around him. The gargoyles shrugged off the flames with apparent disinterest - they were clearly used to fire.

The gargoyles responded by converging on Ursa: 17 damage from one long spear hit (reduced by Sierra's rallying banner resistance), misses from the rest of the round.

Moz cast slow on the red gargoyle - success. Red would lose an action every turn for the next minute, a quiet but critical advantage. Moz burned all three actions on the cast. (Reminder to DM: Moz was greater invisible this entire time - this was forgotten and not tracked until mid-combat.)

Zarius spell-struck the blue gargoyle for a critical hit. Gouging claw triggered bleeding. Arcane cascade activated for bonus damage on subsequent attacks.

Sam (Sierra) used her action economy to aid Zarius with the rallying banner and set up an aid reaction for his next attack.

### Part 3: The Revive Mechanic Discovery
Several rounds in, the party had blue gargoyle nearly dead - and then Zarius downed him. The table celebrated. Then, on the gargoyles' turn, the red gargoyle reached out and drew a glowing rune over the blue one's body. Blue stood back up.

> "Not cool."

Realization: the gargoyles revive each other. Both need to go down in the same round or the downed one immediately recovers. This reframed the entire fight.

The party called it out in-character. Sierra shouted the tactic. Ursa acknowledged the shift. The focus became softening both targets equally rather than spiking one down.

### Part 4: The Grind
The next several rounds were a war of attrition. Key events:

- **Ursa's whirling attacks** continued to deal reliable damage to both gargoyles each round via his area attack (reflex save for half), but the gargoyle saves were frustratingly decent.

- **Sierra's "slip and sizzle"** - Sierra's plan was to have Ursa attempt to trip a gargoyle with her action, setting up bonus damage or advantage. Ursa failed his trip roll nearly every time. One memorable round, he rolled lower than his previous failure. Sierra finally landed one late in the fight.

- **Zarius burned both hero points.** His rolls this session were historically bad. The d100 roll after a hero point reroll was invoked (standard when the reroll also fails) - it didn't help either.

- **Ursa got critically low.** The gargoyles kept directing their attentions at Ursa as the front-liner. He dropped to roughly 11 HP with multiple attacks still incoming. Moz burned an action on sooth to bring him back to 93 HP.

- **Ardryl's gravity weapon** paid off when assisting shot critted - the extra weapon dice damage doubled. His third and fourth actions (haste) helped chip in extra arrow damage each round.

- **Moz's greater invisibility was remembered.** Around round 3-4, someone recalled that greater invisibility had been cast at the start. The DM acknowledged it: Moz had been invisible the whole time. Several attacks that should have missed Moz became retroactively moot.

- **Electric arc + dirge of doom** cycled every round from Moz. Dirge of doom (no save - auto frightened 1) stacked onto the gargoyles and stuck after Moz activated their sustained performance power, removing the need to recast it each turn.

- **Gouging claw correction.** Zarius and the DM discovered mid-combat that the gouging claw damage had been incorrectly configured in Pathbuilder - it was doing far more damage than listed. They corrected the math on the fly.

- **Force fang IS a conflict spell** - previously unclear, confirmed this session. Using force fang recharges Zarius's spell strike, opening up a force fang → spell strike loop each round.

### Part 5: The Kill Shot
Both gargoyles were finally in the same HP range - one "hurt," one "fucked up." Sierra used a commander ability to grant Moz a free spell cast outside of her own action. Moz aimed a fifth-level fireball to catch both gargoyles.

The fireball rolled 36 damage. Both gargoyles succeeded on their saves (half damage: 18 each). Both had fewer than 10 HP. Both died.

Moz also caught Ursa in the blast radius - Ursa saved, took 17 damage, and further halved it due to fire resistance from his Nephilim heritage. The DM noted they'd sort out exact fire resistance numbers next session.

> "Fireball on my own head saves the day once again." - Moz

The DM noted, almost apologetically, that he'd forgotten to activate the pyramid's gatling gun emplacements - tubular weapons that had appeared on the pyramid's exterior. The party took a moment to appreciate the unrealized threat and moved on.

Session ended at 10:30 PM. Aftermath deferred to next week.

## Lore Discoveries

- **Gargoyle revival mechanic:** The two gargoyles can revive each other. One must spend an action to place a rune over a downed ally, restoring them. Both must be downed in the same round to prevent revival. This is described in-universe as similar to the "Decimal Repeat" mechanic from FF5 (per the DM).
- **Pyramid's exterior emplacements:** The pyramid has tubular weapons (gatling guns?) that extend from its exterior. These activated in the April 7 session but have not fired in either combat encounter. The DM forgot to use them this session.
- **Ursa's past life connection to the Ring City:** Ursa claims to have witnessed the Ring City's original construction in a previous incarnation. He is investigating the pyramid specifically because it appears to use Ring City technology - and understanding the pyramid may tell him something about that original construction.

## Mechanical Notes

**Key Rules Clarified This Session:**

| Rule | Clarification |
|------|---------------|
| Force fang (Magus focus spell) | IS a conflict spell → recharges spell strike after use |
| Assisting shot (Press trait) | Can be used multiple times per turn under multi-attack penalty |
