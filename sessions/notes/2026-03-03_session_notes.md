## Session Summary

### Part 1: Morning at the Inn
The session picked up from last week's cliffhanger: Copper Badger had delivered her first piece of real intelligence at breakfast - four patrol shifts at the governor's manor overnight.

Characters trickled downstairs for breakfast:
- **Moz** was already up and had eaten
- **Jack** requested a tall, warm glass of milk, sipped like coffee
- **Zarius** ordered the full Silverleaf breakfast: fruit, waffles, bacon, sausage, pancakes, eggs, and **black sausage** (blood sausage - turns black when blood cooks; confirmed non-poisonous by the waiter)
- **Ardryl** grabbed some fruit and sat down quietly
- **Sierra** slept in; arrived later in her pajamas, wandered to the buffet

Zarius's morning ritual: a calm talk with his sword. Asked it what it wanted to kill today. The sword replied: *"Evil."* The party debated at length whether a sword can meaningfully define evil - concluding that evil is simply "whatever I am not" - and then went on a tangent about fantasy book recommendations (Warbreaker, Mistborn, Red Rising, Darth Plagueis, Darth Bane, X-Wing novels).

### Part 2: Planning the Day
The party debated their approach:

- The **robot dog (Tau?)** has only been spotted at the manor **at night**, not during the day
- The governor's manor doubles as his office - a White House-style arrangement where he both lives and works
- Daytime is still dangerous (staff present), but nighttime is better for a break-in since only guards remain
- **Decision:** Stake out the manor during the day to map patrol patterns, then plan the actual infiltration

Ardryl applied his disguise before heading out (used a hero point on the roll).

Zarius, meanwhile, tried to find an underground criminal contact by asking around. His 38 underworld lore check pointed him to **Ivan's Wonder Emporium** - Ivan/Evan, their already-known smuggler contact. He decided not to pursue it since the resistance was staying there and he didn't want the confrontation. Dead end, but confirmed Ivan is the city's central criminal hub.

Sierra attempted to get intel from the innkeeper:
- How many people work at the governor's manor? *"Go knock on the door and ask."*
- She got some context: the manor used to be a tourist attraction before the mind flayers arrived ~1.5 months ago. The city has 5,000+ residents. Many have been offered "employment" at the pyramid.
- Sierra stalked off to get dressed after receiving no useful information.

### Part 3: Staking Out the Governor's Manor
The party headed to the manor and took up rooftop positions to observe.

**What They Saw:**

The manor is **massively fortified**, even by daytime:
- **Exterior:** Multiple barricades along the road, each with groups of 4 guards
- **Walls:** ~15 feet high, with guards positioned on top
- **Grounds:** Guards patrolling inside the wall perimeter
- **Guardhouse:** Inside the wall, where patrol guards rotate in for breaks
- **Total daytime guards: over 100**

**Building Layout** (Sierra, perception 33):
- **3 stories tall**, approximately 100 feet wide × 60 feet deep
- **Left side (from outside):** Dining room, study, library, meeting room
- **Right side:** At least 4 offices

**Guard Patrol Patterns** (observed over ~1 hour):

| Guard Type | Pattern | Schedule |
|------------|---------|----------|
| Interior guards | Move room-to-room in sequence | 45-minute rotation, starting on the hour |
| Exterior guards | Rotate counterclockwise around the perimeter | 45-minute rotation, starting at half-past the hour |
| Wall/ground patrol | Walk the wall perimeter until reaching the guardhouse | Break at guardhouse, then resume |

Note: the interior and exterior guard rotations are **offset by ~30 minutes** from each other - a complication for any infiltration plan.

**Guard Composition** (Jack, perception 28):
- Mix of humans, orcs, elves, and dwarves
- No half-squids or mind flayer hybrids visible
- Front-line guards (closest to the road): more professional, alert
- Rear guards: more relaxed, chatting, smoking

**Copper Badger Spotted:**
Jack spotted Copper Badger in an alleyway with a small spyglass, watching the manor. She was humming a catchy tune that passersby were unconsciously picking up and repeating. No one seemed to notice or care. Better tradecraft than expected.

**No Sign of Tau:**
The robot dog has only been spotted arriving at night with mind flayer ships. No sign of it during the day.

### Part 4: Planning the Heist Questions
Mid-afternoon, the party agreed to visit Ivan/Evan to gather heist intelligence. Sierra drafted a list of questions to have Moz (the high-charisma party face) ask:

1. Has anyone broken into the manor before?
2. Are there any known secret passages in or out? (Governors often have escape routes)
3. Is there anyone on the staff who can be bribed or blackmailed?
4. Who handles the catering/cooking?
5. Can we get official stationery to forge documents?
6. Any magical or technological wards/protections known?

DM pre-cleared some questions as non-starters:
- **No official events** - city is under occupation
- **No opposed political figures** - all co-opted by the mind flayers
- **No foreign diplomatic visits** - mind flayers don't want anyone in

Remaining questions (broken in before, escape routes, blackmail material, cooking staff, forged documents, wards) will be addressed next session when the party actually speaks to Ivan.

**Item Wish Lists:**
DM reminded the party they can send him item wish lists (for both purchases and treasure). Items of interest mentioned:
- Greater striking runes (party-wide desire, money permitting)
- Climbing/entry gear (kits, spider climb, flight)
- Grappling hook/rope arrow options for Ardryl
- Ardryl's quiver confirmed to work as a **Hawkeye quiver** - can produce creative/novelty arrow types as long as the concept is entertaining

Session ended with Jacob staying behind for a private DM conversation.
