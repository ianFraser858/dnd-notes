# Pathfinder Session Notes - September 15, 2026

## Session Overview
Ardryl opened the door to chase the creature from last session's cliffhanger and woke up the next morning crumpled in the hallway, **down half his hit points with no memory of what happened**. The party found no fur, no tracks, and no magic on the door, so they followed the drunk hunter's lead into the forest outside Keepsake with a rabbit trap, barely scraped through a skill challenge, and spent six hours catching ordinary brown bunnies. At dusk, **white figures began appearing at the edge of everyone's vision** (everyone but Moz), vanishing whenever looked at directly. Ardryl followed his deep into the dark forest to a **glowing sphere containing a scarred, one-eyed, white-furred, multi-armed creature in meditation** — and the session ended as **Sierra ran up and touched the orb**.

## Session Summary

### Part 1: Recap
Emily read the recap from her notes; Joey confirmed it all. Two details were pinned down along the way:
- The **orc blacksmith** is the Greg who matters — "Greg with the extra G on the end" (**Gregg**). Every other unnamed NPC is plain Greg.
- The mayor is **Kato** ("It's definitely Kato," not "Kado").

Daniel claimed Emily "forgot about Da." Joey docked Daniel a hero point and gave it to Emily, plus one more — Emily started the session with **three**.

### Part 2: The Morning After
Picking up from the cliffhanger: Ardryl could not hunt the creature, so he went to the door and opened it to look. Joey called for a Perception check and cut straight to the next morning.

**Moz woke to find Ardryl gone from their room** and found him **crumpled against the hallway wall**. Shaken awake, Ardryl was **missing 50% of his hit points** and remembered nothing after opening the door. He cast **Soothing Mist** on himself (healed 19).

Ardryl's account to Moz: he saw the white rabbit, it said they were an anomaly and wasn't sure whether that was good or bad, it got sucked out the door, he opened the door, and then he woke up on the floor. It did not hurt him as far as he knows.

### Part 3: Waking the Others
Moz (in a red satin / crushed velvet pajama suit) knocked on Jack and Sierra's door. Sierra's first question was whether Ardryl was dead; told no, she tried to go back to sleep. On hearing the full story she drew herself up to all **3'10"** and looked smug — she'd been right that the town was weird.

Investigation of the scene:
- **Ardryl, Nature 29** — was it a rabbit? It was very close to his face and the room was dark. **Maybe. He isn't sure.**
- **Sierra, Arcana 32** (via Untrained Improvisation) — **no magic** on the door.
- **Ardryl, Perception/Survival 31** — **no fur, no tracks, no evidence of any creature** in the hallway or on his bed.

The table recalled that this is not Ardryl's first time: back in the icy region (when Tau was still with the party), he tried to Hunt Prey on something he shouldn't have and was **dragged into the Astral Plane**, where he saw "a great giant looming black presence in the sky" — the **Ancient One**. Sierra: "Are you clairvoyant or something?" Ardryl: "I guess I'm just sensitive."

After some bickering (Sierra pressing Ardryl on why his low-light vision couldn't tell white fur from dark), the party agreed to trust Ardryl and follow the only lead they had: **the forest where the drunk saw the six-armed rabbit**.

### Part 4: Breakfast with Ruby
Jack ordered breakfast to go; Ruby handed over four pancake wraps stuffed with sausage and a hard-boiled egg. Jack asked her two questions:

- **Has anyone disappeared lately?** No. Everyone knows everyone; they'd know. The hunters are gone for days at a time checking traps, but they always come back.
- **Anyone having strange dreams, or feeling a presence in the room?** Only **Greg from the shoe factory** — "a little woohoo" — who is always talking about **a presence around him** and how **"the walls want him."** (Regular Greg. Not the blacksmith, not the guard.)

Ardryl Refocused during the wait and cast Soothing Mist again (healed 23).

### Part 5: The General Store
The two halflings from the bar last night **run the general store**: **Victoria** (the owner) and her helper **Saris**, who communicates mostly by jumping into view and giving a thumbs up.

- Victoria had heard the same rumor: one of the hunters got very drunk and claimed he saw a little six-armed rabbit in the woods. **The hunter's name is Greg.** She doesn't put much stock in it — the hunters are always at least a little drunk.
- She doesn't know where he hunts. Try the **hunters' lodge**, but not early — the hunters stay out late and sleep in.
- Saris produced three traps. The party passed on the neck-breaking one and bought the **box trap** (live capture) for **1 silver**, with a half-price buyback (5 copper) if they return it. They also bought the **large bag of rabbit bait** for **5 copper**.

Table tangent: Pathfinder's printed snare prices are absurd (snare kit 5 gp, trip snare 15 gp, hail of arrows snare is a level 16 item). Joey overrode them. Ardryl didn't build into snares for exactly this reason.

### Part 6: Lost in the Woods (Skill Challenge)
The forest starts about **500 feet** outside town and wraps one side of it — oak, pine, ferns. Ardryl's Forest Lore (rolled a 2): a perfectly standard forest for the region.

**Skill challenge:** Survival, Nature, or Perception. **Six successes before ten failures**; crits count double. Joey: "It should be very easy to accomplish this goal."

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| Jack, Perception | Success — spotted game trails |
| Ardryl, Survival 32 | Success — separated the ungulate trails from the small-critter trails |
| Moz, Perception 27 | Failure — led the group off course |
| Sierra, Perception | Failure — further off course |
| Jack, Perception 34 | Success — recognized a spot on the right trail |
| Ardryl, Forest Lore | Failure — lost again |
| Sierra, 33 | Success — circled back to the trail |
| Jack | Failure — "very lost" (followed the moss-on-the-north-side myth) |
| Ardryl | Failure — still very lost |
| Moz | Success — back on the trail |
| Sierra | Failure — immediately off the trail again |

That put the party at **5 successes and 9 failures** with Jack up. Jack rolled, spent a hero point, then spent another — and the third roll landed. **Challenge passed, 6 to 9.**

They found a cluster of **rabbit burrows**, set the box trap, and found cover to watch from. Ardryl checked for odd tracks (anything six-footed) — too many rabbit tracks to pick anything out. The forest sounded entirely normal.

### Part 7: The Stakeout
Six hours of sitting quietly, which did not come naturally to most of the party.

- **Jack** "pulled a Zarius" and meditated on the **Mana Blade**'s essence (35). For a millisecond the blade registered Jack's presence — but it is thousands of years old, extremely bored, and was absorbed in an endless loop of **Baby Shark** (fifteen times in three seconds), followed by **Found a Peanut**. Jack took **2 psychic damage** and put it away. Joey's forecast for the next check-in: the blade will be streaming Resident Evil 4 on hardcore.
- **Sierra** fidgeted and tinkered. (Her arm comes off, and takes a minute or two to reattach — she doesn't sleep in it.)
- **Moz** meditated, opening one eye to glare at whoever was talking.
- **Ardryl** watched the trap, shushed everyone, and dispatched the catch.

Result: **about twelve ordinary brown bunnies.** No white rabbit.

### Part 8: White Figures at Dusk
The party decided to pack up and try the hunters' lodge. As Ardryl bent to collect the trap, **he caught a white figure in the corner of his eye** and made it his **Hunted Prey**.

- When he focused on it (Perception 33), **it vanished**. Where it had stood there were **no marks on the ground at all**.
- A second look (Perception, **crit success**): **two white figures**. They vanished when he turned toward them.
- **Sierra (34)** caught one in the corner of her eye too, and cast **See the Unseen**: **no illusions, nothing invisible.** The spell's incorporeal detection **"twitched"** — the magic believes these things *should* be incorporeal and tries to treat them that way, but can't resolve them.
- **Jack (34)** caught two more while following.
- **Moz saw nothing**, the entire time.
- Everyone who saw figures was **looking in a different direction**. Assuming Ardryl's was heading north (deeper into the forest), Jack's were drifting northwest and southeast and Sierra's southwest — all moving *away*, never closer.

Every time Ardryl glimpsed his, it was deeper in the trees. He walked after it, laser-focused, no longer hearing the others. The party followed him rather than split up. It got dark; nobody lit a torch; it felt like hours.

### Part 9: The Orb
Ardryl spotted a **glowing dome** off to the right of his path — bigger than a Small creature, smaller than a Medium one (roughly four feet). He crept up (Stealth 41) with no reaction from it. Up close it was a **translucent glowing sphere**, and inside it:

- A creature covered in **white fur**, with **puffy cheeks** and **two long ears**
- Seated in a **meditative pose**: one set of arms down at its belly, another set up over its heart
- **Covered in scars**, and **missing an eye**

Ardryl sat down and tried to look non-threatening. His private read: this thing might be like Gandalf — blamed for the calamity when it's only there to warn people. The rest arrived in order: Sierra (walking, not sneaking), Moz (invisible), Jack (Stealth 31). Ardryl held up a hand for them to stay back. Sierra rolled her eyes.

Nothing happened for a minute or two. Ardryl said, "Hello, friend." **No response.** He backed off to find a stick to prod the sphere with —

**— and Sierra ran forward and put her hand on the orb.** Session ended there.

## Lore Discoveries

- **Whatever is behind the bedroom door costs half your health and your memory.** Ardryl opened the door after the creature was pulled through and lost 50% of his HP and the rest of the night. No magic lingers on the door and nothing physical was left behind.
- **The White Rabbit leaves no physical trace.** No fur on the bed it sat on, no tracks in the hall, no marks in the forest where the figures stood.
- **The creature in the orb matches the eyewitness — mostly.** The hunter described six arms and an eye patch; the party saw two sets of arms in the meditation pose and a missing eye. Its body is covered in scars. Whether a third pair of arms was simply out of view is unconfirmed.
- **The white figures confuse detection magic.** See the Unseen tried to classify them as incorporeal and couldn't. They are something the spell doesn't have a category for.
- **Moz can't see them.** Three of four party members saw figures; Moz never did. No explanation yet.
- **Keepsake has had no disappearances yet.** Per Ruby, the town is intact. The one local oddity is shoe-factory Greg and his "presence."
- **Ardryl has a history with this sort of thing.** His earlier Astral Plane encounter with the Ancient One came up as a possible reason the rabbit singled him out.
- **Keepsake has a hunters' lodge and a shoe factory**, in addition to the mines, forges, bar, and general store.
- **The Mana Blade is thousands of years old and bored out of its mind.**
