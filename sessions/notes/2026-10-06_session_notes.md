## Session Summary

### Part 1: Recap
Emily recapped last session from her notes and got a hero point for it. Joey added one detail: the creature was in a meditative state when Sierra touched the orb. Sam's position: touching the orb was "very brave and sexy of me" and the party is welcome for the plot advancement. Joey moved Sierra's token toward the dead characters folder.

### Part 2: The Orb Is Touched
In the space of a blink:
- The glowing orb **vanished**.
- The party heard a heavy thud against a tree about 20 feet behind them — the sound of a child-sized body hitting a trunk.
- That was **Sierra**. The creature had her pinned against the tree by the throat, lifted off the ground.

Seen clearly for the first time, he is **Medium-sized**. The arms on his left side had **coiled around each other into one large muscular arm**, which was holding Sierra; his other arms were cocked back, ready to pummel her.

Sierra tried to speak ("Hey, big guy, come here often?"). It came out as choking. She then considered dropping a bomb on her own square. Joey gave the numbers — **Escape DC 42, AC about 35** — and the table talked her out of it. (The debate over whether touching the dome counted as entering his personal bubble went against her.)

### Part 3: "How Did You Find Me?"
The creature looked over the group and growled the question.

- **Moz**, still invisible, intoned that they had heard his call, great spirit. Reply: *"You can drop the act, gith boy."* Moz: "What's a gith? Also, we mean you no harm." Reply: *"I'll be the judge of that last part."* Moz was "lying gith boy" for the rest of the session.
- The party explained that Ardryl followed some fleeting shapes and the rest of them followed Ardryl.
- *"Which one of you is Ardryl?"* The half-elf. *"You are Ardryl. You now speak for this clan."* From here on the party is **Clan Ardryl** and Ardryl is its leader, as far as he is concerned.

When Ardryl asked him to at least loosen his grip, he squeezed tighter — Sierra was his bargaining chip.

Ardryl's account: a white rabbit woke him last night in Keepsake, called him an anomaly, and disappeared; he saw it again in the woods and followed it. The creature's reaction: *"Of course he said that. That's what I would say."* **He dropped Sierra.** (Sam, with darkvision and a close view, saw the arms on his right side coil together into a single arm as he let go.)

Ardryl asked if the small rabbit was him, or an evil twin. In answer, **the white figures the party had been glimpsing in the forest coalesced around them as solid bodies** — all of them him, in different states: one a military leader, one younger and less worn, one heavier-set with no scars and both eyes. **Different potential versions of the same individual.**

He took everyone's names. On "Jack BeQuick": *"Your nursery rhymes are beneath me."* He said he is over three thousand years old.

Asked what brought them to the town, the party said rumors that bad things happen wherever a white rabbit goes. *"Your information is accurate. Do you blame the white rabbit for these disappearances?"* The party: correlation is not causation. *"An excellent and correct assumption."*

### Part 4: Questioning Ardryl Alone
He sent Sierra for firewood, Jack for stones to ring a fire, and Moz to help — he wanted the leader alone. A burst of energy came off him and flattened a clearing in the underbrush where the dome had been.

He then walked Ardryl through the last day in detail:
- **The small rabbit's exact words.** Jack had them written down: *"You are an anomaly. You're not supposed to be here. Is this a good thing?"* He confirmed that is exactly what the rabbit would say.
- **What happened after.** The rabbit was pulled out through the door; Ardryl went to look and was knocked out. This got a reaction: *"Wait — you were knocked out?"*
- **The morning.** Ruby served breakfast. *"Where was the bartender?"* Nobody remembers seeing Tim that morning. At the general store they spoke with Victoria and Saris.

His conclusion: *"So far everything is the same except for you being here. ... Everything is progressing as previous. Except for you. Fascinating."*

And then: ***"In 36 hours that town will be dead."*** Sierra screamed the number back at him from across the clearing.

### Part 5: The Campfire
Ardryl (Master in Survival, no roll needed) built the fire. The creature fetched two logs for seating, uncoiled his arms, sat, and let go — and the party watched **the age leak out of him** as the energy of the last few minutes drained away.

His first question was for them: ***"Where is the hellmouth on this planet?"***

There isn't one, as far as the party knows — and they agreed that if there were, it would be a very big problem. They do know what a hellmouth is, from the simulation of another planet they once went through (the one with "the French dude"), a world that was consumed by hellmouths. That they know the word but have no hellmouth to point to fascinated him and, he said, bodes poorly for this world.

### Part 6: Tupi Lulu's Story
- **He is not three thousand years old. He has lived three thousand years.** He is cursed with an affliction that sends him back in time.
- **Origin.** On his home planet he was a simple hunter, supplying furs and meat to his town. He was away on a hunt when a demon struck and devoured the town over three days. He came back, fought it alone, and lost. As he lay dying, a cackling voice cursed him — roughly, *"repeat this day until you end your own."*
- **Turning the curse.** It took a few hundred years to understand it, and a few hundred more to turn it into a tool. He learned to break free temporarily and wind his own time back deliberately, which let him follow the demon to the next town — where he got, in the local phrase, "shit wrecked." That cycle repeated.
- **His planet.** After about two months, the demon had devoured **half the population**. It then left through "a mouth full of fire" — a **hellmouth**.
- **The chase.** He spent a few hundred years learning to travel through hellmouths ("zero out of ten stars") and has since acquired a ship — what locals would call a **spelljammer**. He has now chased the demon across planets for about **twenty real years**.
- **The enemy.** A **memory demon**. It is attacking this planet, and right now this town. It works for the **progenitor of his curse**, who is nowhere near here as far as he knows. He says "demon" loosely; he doesn't know whether his curser is a demon, devil, or daemon. (Hellmouths release all three, mostly demons.)
- **The record.** He has fought it for three millennia and lost every time. He believes he is close: *"Soon I will kill it. Probably on the next planet."* That is not good news for this one — unless the anomaly changes things.

**Where things stand in Keepsake:**
- This is his **second run-through** of this town. He only gives each town a limited number.
- This is **town three** on this planet. After about **five towns** the demon has fed enough that nothing he does can hurt it.
- The demon is **weakened by hellmouth travel** and has to feed to recover, so this is the window. He usually catches up after a town or two; here he has kept pace.
- He has **never found its lair** inside a town — "quite a tricky little bugger when it's small."
- If Keepsake fails, he rolls time back to collect the party and they try the next town. If that fails, about **half the planet dies**.

> *"We can find it. We can stop it. We can make it bleed. And when it bleeds, we can kill it."* — with a manic look in his eyes.

### Part 7: Questions and Answers

**How to fight it**
- He has only ever hurt it by hitting it very hard, so the plan is to hit it harder. He has never seen it bleed.
- No known weaknesses.
- It eats people (teeth), bashes with its body, and cuts with claws. He has never seen it cast, but he doesn't know how it erases memories, so it has something.

**How the rewind works**
- He can restart from the beginning of a day, up to about **three days back**.
- He no longer has to reset everything — he can reset **just one place**. That is why outsiders can walk into a reset area with their own history intact, and why the party registers as an anomaly.
- He limits how many times he resets a place, because letting it fall too far behind the rest of the world causes "truly grisly effects."
- Resetting the **whole timeline** is possible but he won't: that is the curse winning, not him controlling it.
- People in a reset aren't having memories erased; those events simply never happened to them. Asked whether that makes him a memory demon too, he allowed it wasn't an inaccurate description.

**What the demon does to its victims**
- When it consumes someone, **everyone's memory of that person goes with them**.
- **Physical evidence stays.** A consumed household leaves a clean house with food in the pantry; neighbors just think of it as the abandoned house.
- Survivors' minds fill the gap. If it takes a parent, the children still exist — they have no memory of that parent, or a vague story ("went out for milk").
- This matches what Billy Butler described in the first two towns: nothing packed, nothing disturbed, nobody left.

**Why this planet**
He doesn't know how targets are chosen. The demon shows up in one of three roles:

| Role | What it means | His odds |
|------|---------------|----------|
| **Vanguard** (harbinger) | Released before the first small hellmouth opens, to thin the population | Best chance to kill it |
| **Reckoning** | Demons are already arriving; it is turned loose on a weakened world until it gives up | He has tried; it rarely goes well |
| **Message** | Dropped on a major hub city of a rebelling slave-world so everyone watches the city vanish; broadcast | He leaves — nothing to be done |

It doesn't always get to half the population. Sometimes the planet concedes to the demons first.

**Other**
- The party asked if he came down "in a shiny metal tube." He called that an **Iron Rain** and was startled they knew of it; the party confirmed the planet has no such technology.
- Why "clan"? Because they are in Ardryl's clan and he chose Ardryl as the wisest. Adventuring parties aren't a concept he uses; he rarely speaks with locals. He offered to use smaller words for Sierra.
- His name: **Tupi Lulu**. It translates to "white rabbit" (strictly "pearl rabbit," and the two words come from two different languages).

The session ended at the fire with questions still open on both sides. He intends to question the party next — they fascinate him.

## Lore Discoveries

- **The White Rabbit is the hunter, not the omen's cause.** He turns up in doomed towns because he is tracking the thing that dooms them.
- **The vanishings are a memory demon feeding.** Victims are devoured and forgotten; their homes and belongings are left exactly as they were.
- **This world is being softened up for a demonic invasion.** If the demon is here as a vanguard, a hellmouth is expected to open afterward. No hellmouth is known on the planet yet.
- **The pattern on other worlds:** roughly half the population eaten over a couple of months, unless the planet surrenders first.
- **Hellmouths connect worlds** and can be traveled, unpleasantly. They release demons, devils, and daemons — mostly demons.
- **Slave-worlds exist.** The "message" role implies planets already under demonic rule that get punished publicly for rebelling.
- **Localized time resets are real and have limits.** A reset area drifts behind the rest of the world; push it too far and the effects are "grisly." Outsiders are unaffected, which makes them wild cards.
- **The white figures in the forest were his alternate selves.** That explains why detection magic couldn't classify them. (Why Moz couldn't see them is still unexplained.)
- **"Iron Rain"** is his term for arriving by metal tube from the sky. He did not expect anyone here to know the idea.
- **Why the party matters (from Joey, out of character):** everyone on the planet was hit by the **Omniversal Genesis Litany**, which knocked every strong adventurer back to level 1. The party are simply the strongest people left, which is why they are the variable that could tip a reset.
- **Spelljammers:** his ship is the same kind of vessel as the **Fortune of the Trees**, which travels through space into hyperspace and from there to other planes and parts of the universe.

## Plot Developments

### The Mission Has Changed Shape
"Investigate the White Rabbit" is now "find and kill a memory demon hiding in Keepsake within 36 hours." The rabbit is an ally of sorts, and the stakes have gone from one town to half the planet.

### A Clock and a Safety Net
Keepsake dies in 36 hours on the current run. Tupi Lulu can reset the town, but only a limited number of times, and this is already run two. After roughly five towns the demon is beyond him.

### The Party Is the Variable
Everything in Keepsake has played out as it did on the previous run, except for the party's presence. Two things stood out to Tupi Lulu as he questioned Ardryl: that Ardryl was **knocked out** after the rabbit's visit, and that **Tim was not seen** the next morning.

### A Bigger Threat Behind the Mind Flayers
A demonic power with reach across multiple planets has a vanguard on this world. How, or whether, this connects to the mind flayers and the Ring City is unknown.
