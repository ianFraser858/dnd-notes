### Part 3: Debrief with Billy Butler — and a New Name
Ardryl, Moz, and Jack were called into Billy Butler's office. They reported on Horbach:

- They found him, but **he wasn't himself** — mind-controlled, seemingly wanting to be with the squids.
- He **escaped through a weird portal** that appeared to be **Feywild-related**. ("Oh shit, that's bad.")

Billy is visibly **overwhelmed by the end of the world**. He agreed to reimburse the party's expenses, confirmed they still have their **sending stones**, and said he'd try contacting some **drow friends** for intel on the White Rabbit — well outside his wheelhouse.

### Part 4: Choosing the White Rabbit
The party formally chose the **White Rabbit** over rescuing Horbach. Rationale: *"Horbach is one man, but we could get several men... yes, draining men, some might say."* Following the "white rabbit" is judged the higher-leverage lead.

**White Rabbit intel from Billy Butler:**
- Last seen outside the town of **Keepsake**, ~two days from the Hanging City (Billy said "east," later narrated as "west" — chalk it up to the world's usual inconsistencies).
- **Keepsake is the third town** the White Rabbit has been sighted at. In the first two, the **buildings still stand but every person is gone** — and **all livestock and pets too**. All living creatures.
- It does **not** look like a rapture (no meals left mid-eaten) and **not** like they fled (no signs of leaving in a hurry, no home-cooked meals abandoned). Their belongings are simply still there.
- None of the missing have **turned up anywhere else**, and **no one has returned** to the emptied towns.
- The rabbit is a **leading indicator / omen**, not the direct cause — "something wicked this way comes." Seeing it doesn't teleport you; it's a prophetic sign that calamity is coming.
- Only **Nightmare informants** have noticed the rabbit, not common folk. No confirmed description beyond "white rabbit" (Billy couldn't say if it's larger than normal, etc.).

### Part 6: Scouting Keepsake from the Hill
Keepsake: a **mid-sized mining town of ~200 people** with a shoddy, ~10-foot, three-section wall — pure **"deterrence theater"** (a good axe could take it down in ~20 minutes; it just makes the town look inconvenient to raid).

**Moz's Perception 33** revealed the town's layout:
- **Farmland** behind the town.
- **Holes in the earth / ore veins** nearby — a **mine** just outside town.
- **Two large smithing areas** with people milling about.
- A slightly upscale, freshly-painted **house in the center** — probably an important person's (mayor's).
- One building people **go into and leave within ~5 minutes** (the general store).
- One building people **go into and stay ~20 minutes or don't leave** (the bar).
- A few **town guards** with an "authority" look.

### Part 7: The Gate Guard ("Greg")
At the gate, a guard stopped them, immediately mistaking **Moz** (githyanki) for a "super forest elf" / "renel." He directed them to:
- The **bar** (run by **Tim**),
- The **general store** (run by **Victoria**),
- The **mayor** in the central house.

The DM initially named the mayor "Tim," then corrected: **Tim is the bar owner**; the **mayor is Kato, a leshy** (not an elf). The town **sheriff is Alan** — "little guy, big voice, you can hear him yelling from anywhere."

The guard insisted there's **no crime** in Keepsake — big criminals are simply **executed**, which deters everyone; last arrest was ~5 months ago (a bread thief who now works at the general store to pay it off). The party never got his name, so the table named him **"Da,"** which the DM converted into a running gag: everyone whose name doesn't matter is **"Greg,"** but this one is **"Greg with three G's"** (important, unlike ordinary two-G Gregs).

- The party riffed extensively about **establishing organized crime** / a **protection racket** to flush out any hidden underworld ("joining the war on crime, on the side of crime"). Not seriously pursued (yet).

**Orders:** Moz — pear-gorgonzola salad, no drink. Ardryl — elven wine + "bread pizza" (a margherita flatbread). **Eavesdropping (per character):**

Options weighed: relocate to Sledgehammer (rumored already squid-occupied — bad) or **reinforce the mines into bunkers** (misheard as "minds") and stockpile rations down there. ### Part 11: Closing Time & the Drunks (The Real Lead)
As the night wound down, the important patrons filtered out over ~30 minutes, leaving the party, **three drunks**, and the two halflings. Jack chose to eavesdrop on the drunks — **critical success** (they're loud):
- **Drunk 1:** miffed no one investigated his **bitten cow**.
- **Drunk 2:** wants rid of his **five colanders** and needs **cheesecloth**.
- **Drunk 3 (heavy "Alabama" accent):** rambling that he saw a **six-armed rabbit** wandering in the woods — **cute, little, with an eye patch.** The other two dismissed him as too drunk and dragged him home; he passed out on the way out.

This is the party's first firsthand eyewitness description of the White Rabbit: **six arms, an eye patch, small.**

### Part 12: Lodging & the Cliffhanger
The bar had **two rooms, three beds total**, so someone sleeps on the floor (cushions/blankets were already laid out). Sleeping arrangement:
- **Jack** took the bigger room but slept on the floor cushions.
- **Sierra** shared a room, also on the floor.
- **Moz and Ardryl** shared the two twin mattresses in the other room.

Tim charged **15 silver** for rooms + the tab (paid with 2 gold, got 5 silver change). Everyone rolled Perception (Sierra 33) and bedded down.

**During the night, Ardryl felt something (~30-40 lbs) climb onto his chest.** He tried to yell for Moz — but **his voice echoed back at him as if he were in a canyon** (a reality-warp). He opened his eyes: a **furry, whiskered, long-eared creature**, its face directly in his, said:

> *"You weren't an anomaly... you were not supposed to be here. Why is there an anomaly here? Is the anomaly good? Am I late? Am I early? ... This is not what happened last time. Something good is happening this time. Something is going to change this time. ... An anomaly — I love anomalies, anomalies are great."*

The creature was then **yanked off Ardryl and whisked out the door**, which **slammed shut.** Ardryl only made out the eyes, whiskers, and long ears. **Session ended here** (ran slightly over time).

## Lore Discoveries

- **The White Rabbit is an omen, not a mechanism.** Its appearance precedes a town's entire population — people, livestock, and pets — vanishing without any sign of flight or rapture. Keepsake is the third such town. No missing person has ever turned up elsewhere, and no one returns to the emptied towns.
- **Firsthand description of the White Rabbit:** furry, long-eared, whiskered, **small**, with **six arms** and an **eye patch** (drunk's account + Ardryl's brief look). It can **warp reality** (Ardryl's shout echoed as if in a canyon) and it **speaks**, fixating on Ardryl as an "**anomaly**" — something that "wasn't supposed to be here" and that makes "this time" different from "last time." Strongly implies the rabbit has **witnessed / caused this event before** and perceives some kind of loop or repetition.
- **"Delstah"** — a figure **dead ~500 years** — was apparently named by the compromised Horbach (Ardryl's crit-failed Recall). Billy Butler suspects the mind flayers have **taken/controlled Delstah** the same way they took Horbach, possibly via the **Feywild** (Horbach's escape portal was Feywild-related).
- **Keepsake is a mining town** with active ore veins feeding two forges. Governed autonomously but logistically under **Sledgehammer**.
- **Worldbuilding aside:** the setting's **dwarves are insectivores** (bug farmers/eaters). **Orcish beer** exists and is popular among dwarves; **dwarven ale** has dried up since the **Throne/Seat of the Thunder King** shut its trade routes and sided with the squids.
