# Pathfinder Session Notes - February 10, 2026

## Session Overview
The Dreamcatchers explored Silverleaf, visiting the Golden Goose tavern where they learned about the black armband assassins' presence in the city. A helpful waiter directed them to an abandoned warehouse, where they discovered a fledgling resistance movement led by a dwarf named Gold Deer. The mysterious tail from last session turned out to be Copper Badger, the resistance's intel gatherer. The party gathered critical intelligence about the pyramid, mind flayer operations, and a robot dog companion, and decided to target the government building next.

## Session Summary

### Part 1: Recap and Food Run
The session opened with a recap of the previous week: the beetle rider battle, arrival in Silverleaf (correction: the region is Silvermoor, with Silverleaf being one of four city-states), Ivan's stingy hazard pay, the discovery of Ardryl's bounty, and the mysterious tail following the party.

The party debated whether to track the tail or get food. Food won. Sierra rolled a 35 society check and followed her nose to **The Golden Goose**, a busy tavern/restaurant. Jack intimidated a solo diner out of their seat to secure a table.

### Part 2: The Golden Goose
The waiter took their orders quickly:

**Food:** Meatballs covered in gravy, wrapped in pita, with a side of vegetables (dish of the day)

**Drinks:**
- Highland beer: Moz and Zarius (turned out to be vodka-strength - fortitude saves required)
- Regular beer: Ardryl (actually just beer)
- Fruity orange cocktail: Sierra (with fruit skewered on the straw)
- Water: Jack

Zarius ordered a second highland beer, plus trio platters of bread, meat, and cheese bites for the table. The bill came to less than a gold piece.

**Drinking Note:** In this world, "beer" is a catch-all term. Orcish beer = moonshine. Highland beer = vodka. Regular beer = actual beer.

### Part 3: Tavern Revelations
Jack spotted **people wearing black armbands** in the tavern - members of her old assassin company. She warned the group, noting it was suspicious they kept showing up in the same places as the mind flayers. This prompted a tense roleplay exchange about trust:

- Sierra stated she had no *plans* to betray the group, but deliberately didn't say she never would
- Discussion of Teo's betrayal - he was "just following orders" when he poisoned someone
- Zarius expressed skepticism that Keggy is truly gone, citing how many times "that man" has died
- Zarius apparently raved about Keggy while drunk once (he doesn't remember this)

Before leaving, the party finalized Ardryl's disguise: Jack punched Ardryl in the eye, then they applied the disguise kit over the fresh shiner.

### Part 4: The Waiter's Tip
The party asked the waiter about places to stay. He was genuinely unhelpful about hotels ("I'd have my family stay at my family's place"). However, he then asked how far "out of town" they were and offered to introduce them to some people.

He left an address on the bill. Zarius found a gold piece on the floor (rolled a natural 1 on perception - crit fail turned into lucky find). He left it as the waiter's tip.

### Part 5: The Abandoned Warehouse
The address led deep into the warehouse district, far from Ivan's place. Jack cased the joint:
- Cast **See the Unseen** through her eye - no illusions or invisible creatures detected
- Rolled perception (Emily) - nothing suspicious outside, but clearly in an industrial area
- Peeked through windows - everything covered in tarps, building appeared abandoned

Zarius walked straight in without checking for traps ("I trust them. And you guys are behind me. So if I die, you'll protect me."). Inside: stairs going up (confirmed empty rooms Jack saw from outside) and stairs going down.

Going down, they found a long dark hallway with a light at the end. Zarius knocked on a closed door and launched into a rambling explanation of how they got there - without knowing the waiter's name or the tavern's name (the Golden Goose - none of them had looked at the sign).

### Part 6: The Resistance
The door opened to reveal **five people**, including the person who had been following the party. The mysterious tail was **Copper Badger**, the resistance's intel gatherer.

**Gold Deer**, a dwarf, introduced himself as the leader of the resistance. Key details:

- The resistance was established **two weeks ago** (same time as the mind flayer takeover)
- Gold Deer heard that people from "them Nightmares" (the Dreamcatchers' organization) were being smuggled in
- Copper Badger was assigned to watch for them - the waiter at the Golden Goose is a potential recruit who helped point the party their way
- They've been recruiting and even made a flyer (the artwork is... poor)

**Resistance Intel on the Pyramid:**
- Serves as the mind flayers' base of operations
- It attracted/transformed city leaders into "mind slaves"
- Zarius recognized the blue energy tinge as **Ring City technology** (rolled 37 underworld lore) - similar to devices that kept the Ring City afloat
- 24/7 security, heavily guarded
- Mind flayer ships land there, stay about an hour, then leave

**Resistance Intel on the Government Building:**
- Governor John Little's holdout
- Also 24/7 security detail
- Sometimes mind flayer ships visit with a **robot dog** and a **dwarf** companion
- The robot dog is likely Tau - the party's former companion who betrayed them

**Resistance Resources:**
- Zinc Boar is making weapons in the back
- Copper Badger will gather more intel and have something concrete within a day

### Part 7: Codenames and Planning
The resistance uses metal + animal codenames. The party adopted names:

| Character | Codename |
|-----------|----------|
| Jack BeQuick | Gun Metal Cat |
| Ardryl | Steel Hawk (wanted "Iron Eagle" but Iron was taken) |
| Sierra | Razzy |
| Zarius | Zarius (already introduced himself before learning about codenames) |
| Moz | Moz |

The session ended with the party deciding their next target: **the Government Building**, not the pyramid.
- Zarius wants to confront the robot dog (Tau)
- Ardryl wants to take out John Little
- Jack thinks it's the safer of the two options
- Sierra went along with the group

Gold Deer told Badger to gather intel and promised a plan by the next day.

## Lore Discoveries

- **The Mysterious Tail RESOLVED:** It was Copper Badger, the resistance's intel gatherer, watching for the party specifically
- **Pyramid technology:** The blue energy tinge matches Ring City technology used to keep their floating city aloft - confirms the pyramid is of mind flayer/Ring City origin
- **Mind slaves:** People sent to the pyramid are converted into "mind slaves" (not proto-squids specifically)
- **Robot dog sighting:** A robot dog and a dwarf have been seen arriving with mind flayer ships at the government building - likely Tau
- **Mind flayer biology:** They genuinely eat brains - come up behind incapacitated victims and consume their brains through the skull (confirmed both in-world lore and by DM)
- **Resistance exists:** A small 5-person resistance cell has been operating in Silverleaf for ~2 weeks
- **Black armbands:** Jack's old assassin company has members present in Silverleaf, raising questions about their alignment with the mind flayers
