## Session Summary

### Part 1: Ivan's Wonder Emporium
The party arrived at Ivan/Evan's warehouse, which the resistance has now taken up residence in. Gold Deer answered the door, cheerfully reporting that Ivan is letting them stay for **5 gold a night each** in exchange for doing work around the warehouse.

On the way to Ivan's office, the party passed rows of crates stamped with freshly painted "DO NOT OPEN" signs. An attempt was made to bribe Gold Deer with two gold to open one - Gold Deer refused (Ivan charges them every time someone opens a crate). Ivan's office: a dwarf buried in a giant ledger.

Brief comedic detour: Zarius tried to pay 1 gold to find out what was in a box. Ivan confirmed there were no sensor detectors in any boxes. Zarius argued this was not information about what was in the box. Ivan argued it absolutely was. Neither side backed down. No gold was returned.

### Part 2: The Heist Intelligence Q&A
Moz presented Sierra's prepared question list. Ivan addressed each one:

**Q: Has anyone broken in before?**
The only group Ivan knows of who has successfully broken into the manor is the **Silent Order** - a mafia organization. The last known break-in was roughly **6+ months ago**, before John Little took power. The expression on Jack's face indicated she knows exactly who the Silent Order is.

**Q: Is there a secret escape route?**
Yes - governors always have one. Ivan does not know where the exit outlet is, but confirmed its existence with confidence.

**Q: Can we bribe or blackmail staff?**
Difficult. The manor runs on **master servants** - a professional class that is essentially uncorruptible. Bribing lower staff is theoretically possible, but the master servants will hear about it within 6 hours, rendering all gained information useless. Using blackmail involving family members is also inadvisable: the master servants association holds long grudges.

**Q: Who handles catering and cooking?**
No dedicated caterer anymore - they were let go during the "emergency change of government officials." The remaining kitchen staff are three chefs who **commute to work and do not sleep on-site**:
- **Frank**
- **Maria**
- **Entranche** (a title/honorary name earned abroad; claims to have been trained by the legendary Feywild chef **Loro**)

One chef is always present at night in case the governor wants a late snack. This is the party's most viable infiltration vector.

**Q: Any magical or technological protections?**
Ivan listed several:
- **Anti-magic rooms** - entire rooms where magic is suppressed
- **Goggles** that negate invisibility
- **Anti-illusion technology**
- **Anti-magic runes** - the big one; these shut off all magic within their range

Importantly, mind flayers don't use magic - they have psionic abilities that are innate, non-magical mental effects. They would not be affected by anti-magic fields. This means the wards are designed to stop everything *except* the mind flayers and their agents.

**Q: Official stationery/forged documents?**
Ivan directed them to the **Hall of Records** for that.

### Part 3: Mind Flayer Lore - Psionics vs. Magic
A significant lore clarification emerged during the wards discussion:

- Illithids/mind flayers do **not** use magic. Their powers are psionic innate abilities - mental effects that are non-magical.
- They actively **shun magic**. Members who seek out magic use are **excised from the hive mind** - an extreme punishment.
- The Illithid the party fought at the end of **Season 1** was a rare anomaly: he turned to magic because it was the only way he could find to free the Ring City, going against everything his kind believed.
- This is why the anti-magic wards in the manor wouldn't affect the mind flayers or their Illithid-aligned agents.

### Part 4: The Loro Tangent
Ivan's mention of chef Entranche claiming to be trained by "Loro" triggered a Nightmare Lore check:

- **Sam (Sierra):** 32 - Pass
- **Emily (Jack):** 34 - Pass
- **Ian (Moz):** 19 - Fail (could not place the name)

**Nightmare Lore result:** LaRoe/Loro is **the best chef in the Feywild**, discovered through the other adventuring party's exploits (Finley's group). The party also knows of **Jean-Pierre** - a Goliath who was the second-best chef in the Feywild and was encountered during the party's time in the far north (with the dragon). Jean-Pierre always vowed he would one day surpass Loro.

**Loro is a goblin.**

Whether Entranche truly trained under Loro is unconfirmed - but if true, it's notable.

### Part 6: The Hall of Records
Following Ivan's direction, the party headed to the Hall of Records to get blueprints and forged document materials. Zarius rolled Society and Legal Lore (both 33) to locate it, earning a hero point for the obligatory "**We live in a society!**"

They arrived at approximately **3pm** - closing time approaching.

Inside: a clerk at a desk surrounded by floor-to-ceiling filing cabinets. They asked to see the history and construction records of the governor's manor.

**The clerk asked why.** The party answered simultaneously with multiple conflicting cover stories. Moz stepped in and claimed to be an off-world architect writing a book about comparative architecture, inspired by their current stay in Silverleaf. Moz rolled:
- **Deception (26, +hero point):** The clerk was skeptical but asked for more details
- **Diplomacy (39):** The clerk was completely charmed - fascinated by the concept of an off-world visitor writing about architecture

The clerk - **Jonathan** - turned out to be sympathetic to the party's actual cause:
- He mentioned he'd seen the resistance's flyer and was thinking about going, but wanted to bring friends
- He mentioned his friend who works at a local restaurant is also interested
- The party subtly warned him to verify the group's intentions before committing
- Jonathan wisely noted he can't express feelings about the "emergency change of government officials" if he wants to keep his job

Jonathan brought out three layers of records:
1. A large binder of **all proposed renovations**
2. A smaller folder of **approved renovations**
3. A thin envelope (handled with cloth gloves, dimly lit by a special gem lantern only) containing **4-5 original blueprints on blue paper** - sensitive documents that will be ruined by any other light source

The session ended with the party beginning to examine the blueprints.

## Lore Discoveries

- **The Silent Order:** A mafia group that previously broke into the governor's manor ~6+ months ago (before John Little's tenure). Jack appears to know who they are.
- **Manor security - anti-magic wards:** Anti-magic rooms, invisibility-negating goggles, anti-illusion tech, and anti-magic runes that suppress all magic in range. Mind flayers are immune since their powers are non-magical.
- **Mind flayers don't use magic:** Their psionic powers are innate non-magical mental abilities. They actively shun magic - those who seek it are excised from the hive mind. The Season 1 antagonist Illithid was a historical anomaly who turned to magic only as a last resort to free the Ring City.
- **Secret escape route confirmed:** An exit passage exists somewhere in the manor. Ivan doesn't know where it comes out.
- **Master servants:** A professional class running the manor who are effectively uncorruptible. The master servants association is long-memoried - do not threaten their family members.
- **Three commuting chefs:** Frank, Maria, and Entranche. One is always on-site at night. No caterer (fired after John Little took over).
- **Loro is a goblin:** Best chef in the Feywild. Also mentioned as known to Jean-Pierre, the Goliath second-best chef the party encountered in the far north.
- **Jonathan's friend at a restaurant:** This friend is also interested in the resistance and could be the same Golden Goose waiter the party met earlier.
- **Original blueprints of the manor exist** in the Hall of Records, stored in a humidity-controlled vault, only safely read under a specific gem lantern.
