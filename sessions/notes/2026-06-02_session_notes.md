The party entered the blue pyramid for the first time, evading two hybrid-illithid patrols on the industrial catwalk level before reaching the administrative upper floor. ## Session Summary

### Part 1: Entering the Pyramid
The pyramid doors opened on their own as the party positioned themselves against the walls. A stream of small repair robots (knee-height) marched out and began working on the defeated gargoyles, completely ignoring the party. With the robots occupied and the doors open, the party slipped inside.

Inside: ash smell, heat, and the distant clang of metal on metal. Ursa led down the entry hallway to a crossroads. One staircase went up, another went down. From below: unmistakable human screaming.

Ursa pointed down and summarized his mission: his mistress believes the pyramid is fusing humans and machines using Nine Hells technology to build an army of damned souls. The party's weapons cache was "probably that way" — pointing up. They split: Ursa went down, the party went up.

### Part 2: Navigating the Catwalk Level
The upper staircase opened onto an industrial catwalk area — catwalks, machinery below, open space. A half-illithid hybrid patrol was blocking the path about 150 feet ahead.

**Plan:**
- Ardryl (Jack — the archer) turned invisible using their one-per-day invisibility rune and fired a distraction arrow to draw the hybrid's attention
- Moz turned invisible using a spell
- Zarius and Sierra attempted to sneak past while the hybrid was distracted

The shot was slightly off — not as loud as hoped, but it made noise. The hybrid turned toward Zarius and Sierra. As they froze, a second hybrid appeared on a lower catwalk and began tracking the first hybrid's gaze.

The hybrids sent a wave of psychic energy washing over Zarius and Sierra. Moz, already past the first hybrid, came back within 30 feet and cast subconscious suggestion on the nearer hybrid — suggesting it ignore Zarius and Sierra the next time it thought of them. The spell landed (hybrid failed its will save). The second hybrid, taking behavioral cues from the first, also looked away.

The party crossed the catwalk without incident. At the next crossroads, they found writing on the wall in Deep Speech (squiggly lines — no one could read it; the script is designed to be communicated telepathically). From above: the unmistakable hum of massive electrical generation.

Sierra recognized it as electricity; the others just heard loud metal sounds. The party chose to go toward the electricity.

### Part 3: The Administrative Level
The pyramid's upper level shifted from industrial to "corporate" — lit rooms with doors labeled in Common. Four rooms in the hallway:

| Door | Label |
|------|-------|
| Left (near entrance) | Teleporter Room |
| Left (far) | Bedroom |
| Right | Security |
| End of hall | Do Not Enter — Very Dangerous |

**Security Room (empty):**
Jack picked the lock (critical success). Inside: a desk, and three walls lined with cases holding black-tube firearms with handles and attached metal boxes — assault rifles, identified by Sierra. The weapons are clearly Starfinder-level technology, far beyond anything in this world.

- 30 bullets per gun; bullets don't match any existing ammo
- Bulk 2 each (heavy)
- No one in the party is trained in their use; Sierra is familiar with guns conceptually (she has a gun arm) but these are beyond her expertise
- Map on the desk: shows only the top floor and upper stairs — nothing below where the party split from Ursa
- Notes in the desk: musings of a madman
- 1 gold piece found (no one wanted "squid gold")

Zarius, Moz, and Sierra each took one gun. Ardryl and Jack abstained (encumbrance/no room).

### Part 4: The Voice in the Sword
While in the hallway, Zarius heard a muffled voice in the back of his head. As he drew his sword, Dorn — the usual personality — had gone to sleep. Horvox, the second personality, was awake: **"I think dad's here."**

The party understood "dad" to mean Horbach — a living paragon the sword is connected to. The consensus: Horbach is somewhere in the pyramid, likely held prisoner or being coerced into making weapons. He is described as god-like in power; the party debated whether to try to find and rescue him.

### Part 5: The "Very Dangerous" Room
Sierra picked the locked door in three rounds (two successes, one success, one final success — a notably tricky lock). Inside: several display cases, each containing a weapon.

**First attempt (Jack):** Jack reached for a weapon and felt their body begin to deconstruct — used a hero point, barely passed the fortitude check.

**Ardryl's attempts:** Ardryl (arriving slightly behind) reached for the first weapon — a sword — without knowing what had just happened. Their body began to deconstruct; ash came up from their lungs. They let go and lost 50% of their max HP.

Ardryl tried again, wrapping cloth around their hand. The cloth turned to ash. Another fortitude check — another 50% of remaining HP lost. Ardryl ended the session at roughly 25% HP.

**Identifying the sword:**
Sierra used crafting appraisal and rolled a 37 — three times in a row (a 1-in-8,000 chance that the table appreciated).

The sword is **Karen's Claw**, the longsword of **Celeste Killian** — one of the original heroes of the Nightmares, who died during the first raid on the Ring City. Celeste was killed by a Mind Flayer Dragon's breath attack; she was the only hero to die in that battle. Every member of the Nightmares is shown this sword during orientation, which is how Sierra recognized it.

Karen's Claw turns everything it touches to ash — including bags, cloth, and people. Celeste was able to wield it because she was built differently (and likely level 17-18 at the time).

**Gauntlet solution:**
One of the other items in the room was a single gauntlet that absorbs magic. Moz put on the gauntlet and was able to pick up Karen's Claw safely. It now rests on Moz's back alongside the gun.

**Other items in the room:**

| Item | Properties |
|------|-----------|
| Karen's Claw | Celeste Killian's longsword; turns anything it touches to ash (except via the magic-absorbing gauntlet) |
| Gloves (Set 1) | Imbued with spell slots; wearer can cast the stored spells |
| Gloves (Set 2) | Reveals a creature's circulatory system; exact effect unclear |
| Gauntlet | Absorbs magic; currently worn by Moz on one hand |
| Additional items | Multiple other unnamed items — looted into the bag of holding unidentified |

None of the other items were identified as Nightmare-era artifacts beyond Karen's Claw (everything else in the room is unique but unrecognized).

The entire haul went into the bag of holding.

### Part 6: Session End
With the weapons cache secured and Ardryl at critical HP, the party debated next steps: find Horbach (based on Horvox's message) or use the Teleporter Room to escape. The DM called the session at this point — party is in the hallway outside the now-cleared "Very Dangerous" room.

> "He's a living paragon. That's a huge difference." — Sierra

> "What if we take one of those swords and give it to an enemy?" — Sierra

## Lore Discoveries

- **Pyramid interior layout (upper section):**
  - Ground floor: industrial catwalks, machinery (looked grim, party avoided looking down), hybrid patrols
  - Upper floor: administrative area — teleporter room, bedroom, security, weapons vault
  - Lower floor (Ursa's section): human experimentation — fusing people with machines using Nine Hells technology
  - Map in security only covers upper floor and above; lower section is uncharted

- **The Nightmares' weapons are here:** The "very dangerous" room contains legendary Nightmare-era artifacts. Karen's Claw (Celeste Killian's weapon) is confirmed. The others are unidentified but likely also come from the original heroes. How these got to the pyramid is unknown.

- **Celeste Killian:** One of the original eight heroes of the Nightmares. The only one who died during the first Ring City raid — killed by a Mind Flayer Dragon's breath attack. Her sword Karen's Claw was left on the Ring City; it is now in the party's possession.

- **Horbach is in the pyramid:** Horvox (the personality in Zarius's sword) can sense Horbach's presence. Horbach is a living paragon — god-like in capability. He is either a prisoner of the mind flayers, being coerced into making weapons, or (the party's fear) has turned against them.

- **Pyramid purpose confirmed:** The lower levels are being used to fuse humans and machines using Nine Hells technology — creating an army of "damned souls" to serve the mind flayers.

- **The guns:** The security room was stocked with assault rifles — high-tech firearms with no equivalent in this world. They use unfamiliar ammunition. They are likely the "weapons cache" Ursa was looking for on behalf of his mistress.
