# Pathfinder Session Notes - February 17, 2026

## Session Overview
Still in the resistance hideout from last session, the party finally met Iron Fox and learned Governor Little volunteered to serve the mind flayers rather than being mind-controlled. A security audit of the resistance's flyer revealed they had inadvertently burned both their primary and secondary safe locations. After a failed attempt to convince them to move, the party watched the entire resistance march out en masse to a new location while Sierra had a spectacular meltdown about the city's collective incompetence. The session ended with the party in an inn, morning arriving, and Copper Badger delivering the first useful intel on the governor's manor patrol schedule.

## Session Summary

### Part 1: Meeting Iron Fox
The session opened mid-scene at the resistance hideout. The party had heard Iron Fox mentioned but hadn't met them yet. Gold Deer confirmed: Iron Fox uses she/her pronouns, is always in the training room, and could be found in the back.

They found Iron Fox practicing alone - throwing genuinely terrible punches and kicks with no follow-through. Sam rolled a 27 Warfare Lore to identify her as a **Kashrishi** - a rare short rhino-like creature with a crystal horn, almost never seen in this part of the world.

**Sierra's Training Montage:**
Despite not being particularly skilled in unarmed combat herself, Sierra decided to coach Iron Fox on basic form - holding her elbow higher, adjusting her stance. She rolled a **critical success** (20+ over DC). Iron Fox went from level 0 to level 1 monk, now understanding the fundamentals of punch-punch-kick. Sierra intends to make this an ongoing project.

> "I'm going to feel responsible if they die, you know? If I go any further without helping them, I will be an accessory to their deaths."

### Part 2: Intelligence on Governor Little
Jack asked Gold Deer how Governor Little came into power:

**Gold Deer's Account:**
- The mind flayers swept in and installed him as a puppet governor
- He had **proven himself** to the mind flayers beforehand by helping them take over the Seat of the Thunderlord
- Was rewarded with governorship of Silverleaf as payment
- He is the mind flayers' "little bitch" who does whatever they tell him

**Key Revelation:** Governor Little does NOT appear to be mind-controlled. He volunteered. He conquered the Seat of the Thunderlord on behalf of the mind flayers and received political power as his reward.

The party noted the Seat of the Thunderlord is where they had previously been, and where Zarius's talking sword (a rapier) has history. The party's connection to the Seat of the Thunderlord is now confirmed to be entangled with Governor Little's betrayal.

**On the Pyramid:**
Gold Deer confirmed it arrived about two weeks ago. No additional intel beyond what was already known - Copper Badger has been unable to get close due to heavy security.

### Part 3: The Flyer Security Disaster
The party asked to actually look at the resistance's recruitment flyer in detail. Gold Deer proudly handed it over. Contents:

- **Header:** "Join the Resistance!" with a (badly drawn) fist raised against a squid figure
- **Hours:** Come to this location between [hour] and [hour] (20-hour window, leaving only 5 hours for sleep)
- **Primary Location:** The resistance's actual warehouse hideout - with address
- **Secondary Location:** "If you can't find anyone here, come here" - pointing to The Golden Goose tavern

**The Problem:** They burned both their primary AND secondary safe locations on a flyer distributed across the city for 2-3 days.

Moz raised the alarm: suggested a decoy meeting point with a lookout, with the actual operational base elsewhere. The whole party tried to convince Gold Deer:
- Moz rolled Diplomacy: **15** - Gold Deer "would take it under consideration"
- Jack: **Critical fail (1)** - Gold Deer also "would take it under consideration"
- Ardryl: **Failed** - same result
- Someone else pushed it over the line - Gold Deer eventually agreed to the concept

The party suggested using Ivan and Even's warehouse as their new secondary (actual) base. Gold Deer agreed.

### Part 4: The Resistance Marches Out
Without further discussion, the **entire resistance stood up and walked out the door as a group**, heading to Ivan's place. All five of them. Together. Out in the open.

They began chanting: **"Fuck the squids!"**

Sam suggested covering them in alcohol to make them look like drunken idiots wandering the streets rather than a conspicuous resistance cell - which Gold Deer declined to stop for.

The party watched them go. Deliberated briefly. Then decided to just let it happen and head to the governor's manor instead.

> "They're dead. I don't want to see them again because then I'll feel bad about it. I mourned. I want to move on."

### Part 5: Sierra's Crash Out
Attempting to find lodging for the night, the party quickly hit a wall:

- **Condemned buildings:** Society check found some - all in the warehouse district. On the other side of town.
- **Business district:** All buildings currently occupied.
- **The idea of using Ivan's/Even's:** Rejected. The party wasn't going to face the resistance members they just abandoned.
- **Brothels:** Zarius pointed out the red light district was "hopping" because citizens of an occupied city have nothing to do but drink and have sex.
- **Inns:** Where were the bloody inns?

Sierra reached her limit. A detailed cataloguing of indignities followed: the waiter who didn't understand what an inn was, no one in this city understanding basic hospitality, the resistance burning their own safe houses, the whole city being full of morons. She threw bottles at the wall. She was "losing it" and knew she was losing it.

Eventually: Sierra walked into the nearest inn, slammed **10 gold pieces** on the counter, and said *"Give me the keys."*

**Cost:** 2 gold per room. 5 rooms secured.

**The keys did not specify which rooms.** Sierra went upstairs and started trying doors until she found ones that worked. She slept furiously.

### Part 6: Night Watches
The party organized watch shifts. With 5 people watching (Sierra included despite her mood), this took ~10 hours.

| Watch | Character | Events |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| 1st | Sierra | Tinkered to calm down; cast See the Unseen during final hour - nothing detected |
| 2nd | Zarius | Talked to his sword about the day; nightmaid repeatedly weirded out by him standing in the hallway |
| 3rd | Jack | Spotted Copper Badger sneaking down the hall (terrible stealth - ducking into doorframes, doing the Naruto run) |
| 4th | Ardryl | Uneventful |
| 5th | Moz | Uneventful; nightmaid set up early breakfast |

**Copper Badger on Jack's Watch:**
Badger snuck up (badly) to inform Jack that Ivan and Even's place had rooms available for 5 gold a night. Jack said they were fine.

Badger also updated her mission: she's going to investigate the **governor's manor** tomorrow (since that's the party's target), not the pyramid.

### Part 7: Morning and Intel
Moz attended the early breakfast spread: oats, macerated fruit (strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, pineapple), omelet station, bacon and potatoes.

Copper Badger arrived at breakfast to deliver her first piece of real intelligence:

> **The governor's manor has at least 4 distinct patrol shifts during the night.**

This is more useful information than anything the resistance has produced so far.

## Lore Discoveries

- **Governor Little is NOT mind-controlled.** He willingly helped the mind flayers seize the Seat of the Thunderlord and was rewarded with governorship of Silverleaf. He is a voluntary collaborator.
- **The Seat of the Thunderlord** is directly connected to the mind flayer occupation - Little's conquest of it on their behalf was the proof of loyalty that earned him his position.
- **Zarius's rapier:** Confirmed to have history at the Seat of the Thunderlord. It can talk (or Zarius learned to talk to it there). It is distinct from the Manablade (which speaks easily to anyone), requiring deliberate effort to communicate.
- **Kashrishi:** Iron Fox's species - short, rhino-like creatures with crystal horns, extremely rare in Silvermoor.
- **Governor's manor patrol:** 4 distinct night shift rotations confirmed by Copper Badger.
- **Resistance OPSEC:** The flyer revealed both the primary base and The Golden Goose as fallback - both locations now compromised. Party intervened to establish a decoy system.
