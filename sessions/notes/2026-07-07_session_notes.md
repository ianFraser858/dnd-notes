## Session Summary

### Part 1: Recap and Decision — Escape or Find Horbach
Sierra recapped last session: the pyramid layout, the Nightmare weapons cache, the "Very Dangerous" room with Karen's Claw, and Horvox's *"I think dad's here"*.

The party debated whether to leave via the Teleporter Room or push deeper to find Horbach. Consensus quickly formed around Horbach — leaving him to keep forging weapons for the mind flayers was unacceptable, and if he saved them (as Horvox seemed to imply), they owed him.

Sierra tried to Recall Knowledge / warfare-lore where Horbach would be held. Rolled poorly — concluded he'd obviously be "in the deepest level behind the highest security guarded by 1,500 squids at a minimum." The party did not take this seriously.

### Part 2: Sabotaging the Security Room
Before leaving the administrative floor, Sierra returned to the Security Room. Not trained enough to disable the guns properly, she filled four of the assault rifles with glue instead — a "Chekhov's glue-jar" moment (she'd picked it up specifically for this kind of scenario). Four rifles are now non-functional.

### Part 3: The Teleporter Room
Sierra used a Flash of Inspiration meta-request to figure out what to try next, then investigated the Teleporter Room while the glue sabotage finished up.

Inside: **four teleportation pads**. Three had Deep Speech writing on top of them (unreadable). The fourth was labeled in Common:

> **GOVERNOR**

It appears active and could be used. The party did not step onto it. They tabled it as a potential escape route for later.

### Part 4: Descending to the Forge
They returned to the crossroads on the catwalk level and took the passage they had not explored previously. The air grew hotter. Ardryl heard the sound of metal on metal — clearly a hammer on an anvil — and led the party toward it.

The hallway opened into a section of massive parallel pipes running along its length. One pipe was extremely cold (condensation collecting on the outside); the other was extremely hot (steam wafting off it). Cooling water on one side, molten metal on the other — a forge supply line.

The party paused to fully heal before pressing on:
- Sierra, Zarius, and Moz Treated Wounds in a rotation (Ardryl is not trained in medicine and could not participate as a healer)
- Sierra also used a Life Shot (via her arm gun) on someone as a supplement — noted that potions are better saved for combat
- Multiple rounds of Treat Wounds, one critical success, several successes
- Roughly an hour to an hour and a half of in-game time to bring everyone to full HP

### Part 5: The Forge
The hallway ended in a large forge chamber. Directly opposite the party, on the far side of the room, stood a dwarf:

- One arm literally on fire
- Other arm swinging a large hammer
- Steadily working a piece of gear on the anvil

This is **Horbach**. The pipes from the corridor feed the forge and its cooling trough.

Between the party and the forge, laying watchful in the middle of the room, was a **large robotic dog** — noticeably bigger than Tau (their old companion) and bigger than the bipedal laser-cannon variant they fought previously. Ardryl (perception 18) confirmed it was neither of the two robot-dog types they'd seen before.

Zarius asked the sword whether this was really Horbach. Diplomacy 32. Horvox's response: *"It sounds like dad."* (Horvox is currently sleeping/resting — asleep in the way "honkshoe" characters snore in cartoons — but confirmed it before dozing back off.)

### Part 6: Reading the Room
The party stayed at the mouth of the tunnel and assessed:

- Sierra asked whether Horbach looked imprisoned or willing. Perception check: **he does not look imprisoned.**
- Ardryl asked whether Horbach was mind-controlled. Not determinable from this range.
- Sierra used Warfare Lore (**33**) on the dog's posture: it's laying down, staring down the tunnel toward threats — **it's a bodyguard, not a jailer.** It's protecting Horbach, not restraining him.

Conclusion — dreaded but clear: **Horbach appears to be a voluntary collaborator.**

Ardryl, brutal about it: "So we're fucked. Horbach is probably not a friendly."

### Part 7: First Contact
Sierra decided to approach openly rather than stealth (Moz warned that stealthing now would read as a threat). She walked calmly out with hands to her sides.

The dog stood up and watched her (Arcana 32 — hostile posture, not friendly). She kept advancing anyway, tried a cat-style "pss pss pss" — Emily got a hero point for the bit — and finally called out a greeting.

**Horbach's first words, in what sounded like an automated challenge sequence:**

> "Access code?"

Sierra had no access code. Horbach replied:

> "Access denied."

Then, addressing the dog:

> "Breathe this area. You are under attack. I shall hold them off."

He hit a button on the forge. A **portal opened behind him**. The dog began to **transform and get much larger**.

Sierra's last-ditch play: she held up her assault rifle and shouted "We're on your side — we have guns too." Horbach's response, to himself or someone unseen:

> "They have a greater weapon than previously imagined."

**Realization at the table:** the access code is almost certainly obtainable from the Governor's Manor. The party has been planning the manor infiltration for months but hadn't executed it yet. They skipped the intended prerequisite.

Combat is about to begin. Joey rolled initiative and stopped the session — no time to run the fight tonight.

## Lore Discoveries

- **Horbach is voluntarily aligned with the mind flayers.** He is not imprisoned. He works the forge unsupervised, has a bodyguard, and controls a portal system from the forge itself. This overturns the party's working assumption that he was being coerced.

- **Access codes gate the pyramid's higher-level systems.** Horbach's first challenge was "access code?" — the party's inference is that this code was obtainable from the Governor's Manor (John Little's), and they skipped a critical prerequisite by going straight to the pyramid. The infiltration of the manor is now not just an Ardryl grudge — it's a mission prerequisite.

- **The Teleporter Room is a hub, and one pad is publicly labeled GOVERNOR.** The other three use Deep Speech labels. Strong implication: the pyramid and the Governor's Manor are teleporter-linked. That both explains John Little's rapid political reach and gives the party a potential covert route between the two.

- **The forge has a live portal system separate from the Teleporter Room.** Horbach opened one behind himself with a button. Destination unknown — possible reinforcements route, possible escape route.

- **There are at least three distinct classes of mind-flayer-aligned robotic construct:** Tau (small dog), the bipedal laser-cannon type (previously fought), and the large bodyguard type met tonight. The party is now compiling this taxonomy informally.
